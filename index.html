Capital & Daily Target

Honest warning — read before choosing Aggressive

$10–$15 per day on a $200 account is a high-risk target — it is NOT guaranteed, and most traders who chase it lose money. At 2.5% risk, one losing trade costs about −$5, and a bad day can lose −$5 to −$7.50 or more. Two or three losing days in a row can cut the account by 10%+. The scanner only reduces guesswork — it cannot remove market risk. If the daily limit of 3 trades is reached, stop for the day.

How Signals Are Decided

  • Every candidate is scored on trend (EMA 20/50/200 across 15m, 1H, 4H), RSI, MACD, volume, support/resistance, ATR, funding rate, open interest change, long/short ratios, BTC context, liquidity pools and risk:reward.
  • A LONG or SHORT signal needs at least 7 confirmations plus the confidence threshold for your mode — otherwise the result is NO TRADE with the reason shown.
  • Stop loss comes from the recent swing and ATR; targets are set at fixed R multiples (TP1 / TP2 / TP3), capped by the nearest support/resistance wall.
  • Data is fetched live on your device. The app tries Binance Futures briefly, then CoinGecko, Binance Spot (data-api.binance.vision), CoinPaprika, OKX, Bitget, Gate.io and Bybit, and finally the same data through public relays, and clearly labels the active source (for example "Bitget · Live"). Prices may differ a fraction between exchanges — always confirm the exact price on Binance before entering. The Binance app working does not mean the web Futures API will work in a browser — the fallback chain exists for exactly that case, with no VPN needed. No price is ever invented — if every source and relay fails, you get an error with per-source results, not a made-up number.

Risk Disclaimer

This app provides market analysis and trade setups only — it is not financial advice and it never guarantees profit. Leveraged futures can liquidate your entire margin, especially in small altcoins. Always use a stop loss, risk only what your settings allow per trade, and confirm the live price on Binance before entering. The app never trades for you.
